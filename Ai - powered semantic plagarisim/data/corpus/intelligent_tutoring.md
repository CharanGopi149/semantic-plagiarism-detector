# Intelligent Tutoring Systems in Higher Education

## Introduction
Intelligent tutoring systems personalize instruction by adapting examples, hints, and pacing to each learner's demonstrated understanding. Universities often use these platforms to supplement large classes where individual feedback is limited.

## Methodology
Adaptive tutoring models combine interaction logs, quiz outcomes, and response time to estimate student mastery. The system then adjusts the difficulty of subsequent questions and offers targeted feedback when misconceptions appear repeatedly.

## Discussion
Researchers report that students benefit most when automated guidance is paired with reflective activities rather than treated as a replacement for teaching staff. Effective deployments emphasize formative feedback, transparent evaluation, and regular monitoring of bias in recommendation rules.
